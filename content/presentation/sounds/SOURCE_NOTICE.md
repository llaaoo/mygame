# Original demonstration cue sounds — CC0

These short WAVs are original, deterministic sine synthesis created for this project by `tools/build_presentation_assets.py`. They contain no samples or recordings from third parties. They are released under CC0-1.0 and may be used commercially. Full legal text: [CC0-1.0](../../art/third_party/puny/CC0-1.0.txt).

`manifest.json` records the actual hashes. Running the offline builder recreates the WAVs and native sample resources; no network is used. These are small integration samples, not final environmental recordings or a complete music/foley library. Replace a cue's AudioStream in the Godot inspector to use final audio.
